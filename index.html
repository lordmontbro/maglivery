Why Magnetic Tracks?

Discover why magnetic levitation and pathing is the ultimate solution for modern automated delivery.

Maximum Efficiency & Zero Friction

Traditional delivery systems suffer from physical wear, power loss from friction, and mechanical delays. Magnetic tracks eliminate mechanical drag, allowing delivery units to move effortlessly with near-zero energy loss.

Precision Delivery Routing

Magnetic pathing ensures sub-millimeter precision when docking and navigating complex delivery layouts. Goods arrive exactly where they need to be—every single time—without lane drift.

120+ MPH Speed Rating

Ultra-strong neodymium magnet integration keeps delivery shuttles rock solid even at high speeds, handling swift acceleration and tight turns safely.

Zero Maintenance & Contact Wear

Without physical contact or moving track components, there are no belts to snap, tires to replace, or rails to wear out, reducing operational downtime and overall costs.

Paint Safe & Non-Destructive

Soft-grip protective backing protects underlying surfaces and clearcoats from scratches, allowing instant setup and removal anywhere.

Raspberry Pi Powered

Powered by Raspberry Pi integration for smart, scalable real-time routing, effortless compliance, and high-performance automated control.
